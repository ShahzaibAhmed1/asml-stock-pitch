#4 Variant Perception

What the Market Is Missing

Our Thesis

Consensus underestimates ASML's 2028-2030 margin expansion because the earnings impact of High-NA EUV is not yet modeled. High-NA systems sell at ~EUR 350M (vs. ~EUR 200M for standard EUV), with comparable gross margins. As High-NA enters volume production in 2027-2028, it drives both revenue per system and installed-base service revenue higher than the street expects.

Consensus vs. Our View

ConsensusOur EstimateDelta
2028E RevenueEUR 44.9BEUR 47.5B+5.8%
2028E Gross Margin~53%56.1%+310bps
2028E EPSEUR 39.51EUR 41.80+5.8%
2030E Revenue~EUR 50BEUR 54.4B+8.8%

Why the Market Is Wrong

  • 1. High-NA pricing not modeled: Most sell-side models still use blended ASPs that underweight the 75%+ price uplift from High-NA systems.
  • 2. Installed base compounding: Large installed base of DUV/EUV systems globally (ASML FY2025 report). Management guides installed-base management revenue growing at high single-digit rates, supported by high service-contract adoption — a recurring stream the market underweights.
  • 3. Monopoly mispricing: ASML trades at a premium to semi-cap peers, but the premium has actually compressed since 2021 despite the moat widening. EUV has no substitute; High-NA extends the moat by another decade.

What Would Prove Us Wrong

  • High-NA delays: If full manufacturing integration takes >3 years, the 2028E margin uplift shifts out.
  • China revenue cliff: If China drops to ~10% of revenue (vs. our ~20% base case), 2026E revenue falls to ~EUR 34B and 2027E to ~EUR 37B — erasing ~60% of modeled growth. If restrictions extend to servicing installed base, the impact deepens further.
  • Cycle downturn: A severe AI capex pullback in 2027 could defer orders and compress the backlog, making our above-consensus estimates moot.

Sources: Bloomberg consensus (April 9, 2026), ASML Investor Day 2024, Reuters, house estimates

#6 Key Risks

Risk Factors

China Export Controls (Escalating)

HIGH

US proposed fresh restrictions in April 2026 that could further limit ASML's DUV business in China. Analysts estimate up to ~10% EPS impact in a full-restriction scenario. Earlier bans already eliminated most EUV shipments to China; the new risk is DUV tool servicing and spare parts.

Impact: Downside scenario: Revenue decline of EUR 5-6B if proposed restrictions are fully enacted; partially offset by reallocation to other fabs with 12-18 month lag.

Semiconductor Cycle Downturn

MEDIUM

Despite structural demand, the chip industry remains cyclical. An inventory correction could defer EUV orders by 1-2 quarters. EUR 38.8B backlog provides ~12 months buffer but is not immune to cancellations in a severe downturn.

Impact: Downside scenario: 15-20% revenue decline in a severe correction; margins compress 200-300bps. Our above-consensus estimates become the primary casualty.

Valuation Premium Compression

MEDIUM

At ~39x forward P/E versus peer avg of ~24x, ASML trades at a 64% premium. Any growth disappointment could trigger multiple contraction. House-estimate DCF yields EUR 914 vs. market price of ~EUR 1,330 — the gap is the "monopoly premium."

Impact: Reversion to 30x forward P/E implies ~20% downside from current levels.

Rare Earth Supply Disruption

MEDIUM

China controls ~60% of rare earth processing. Retaliatory export controls could delay EUV component manufacturing and shipments.

Technology Disruption Risk

LOW

No viable EUV alternative exists within a 10-year horizon. Minimal near-term competitive threat.

Sources: Reuters (April 2026), US Bureau of Industry and Security, ASML Annual Report 2025
