#6 Appendix

Key Risks

China Export Controls

HIGH

Tightening US/EU restrictions could eliminate ~15-20% of ASML revenue. DUV export bans expanded in 2024. Further restrictions on servicing existing Chinese tools represent incremental risk.

Impact: Revenue decline of EUR 5-6B; partially offset by reallocation to other fabs

Semiconductor Cycle Downturn

MEDIUM

Despite structural demand, the chip industry remains cyclical. An inventory correction could defer EUV orders. EUR 38.8B backlog provides ~12 months buffer.

Impact: 15-20% revenue decline; margins compress 200-300bps

Valuation Premium Compression

MEDIUM

At ~39x forward P/E versus peer avg of ~24x, any disappointment in growth or margins could trigger multiple contraction. Reversion to 30x P/E implies ~20% downside.

Impact: Stock repricing to $1,000-$1,100 range

Rare Earth Supply Disruption

MEDIUM

China controls ~60% of rare earth processing. Supply chain disruption could delay shipments.

Technology Disruption Risk

LOW

No viable EUV alternative exists within a 10-year horizon. Minimal near-term impact.

Sources: US Bureau of Industry and Security, ASML Annual Report 2025, analyst commentary
